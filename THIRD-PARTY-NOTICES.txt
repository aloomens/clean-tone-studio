Algorithms (published research, no code copied)
  Reverb: feedback delay network with frequency-dependent absorption per line after
    J.-M. Jot and A. Chaigne, "Digital Delay Networks for Designing Artificial
    Reverberators", AES 90th Convention, 1991.
  Warmth: first-order antiderivative anti-aliasing of the waveshaper after
    J. D. Parker, V. Zavalishin and E. Le Bivic, "Reducing the Aliasing of Nonlinear
    Waveshaping Using Continuous-Time Convolution", DAFx 2016.
  IR Loader ALIGN: minimum-phase conversion by the real-cepstrum (homomorphic) method,
    A. V. Oppenheim and R. W. Schafer, "Discrete-Time Signal Processing", chapter 5.
  IR Loader: uniformly partitioned overlap-save convolution with a direct-form head
    (zero latency), a standard technique (e.g. W. G. Gardner, "Efficient Convolution
    without Input-Output Delay", JAES 1995).
