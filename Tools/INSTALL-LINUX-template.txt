CLEAN TONE STUDIO {VERSION} - Linux x86-64 (BETA)
=============================================

This is an experimental Linux build. It is the same Clean Tone Studio as the
Windows release, built natively for Linux. It passes the full automated test
suite and loads in Ardour, but it has not yet been tried widely on real Linux
systems. Please tell us how it works for you (see "Feedback" below).

Contents
--------
  VST3/Clean Tone Studio.vst3     The VST3 plugin (a folder - keep it whole)
  LV2/Clean Tone Studio.lv2       The LV2 plugin (a folder - keep it whole)
  Standalone/CleanToneStudio      The standalone app (runs from anywhere)
  install.sh                      Copies both plugins into your home folder
  README.md                       Full manual: signal chain, every control, presets

Requirements
------------
64-bit Intel/AMD Linux with glibc 2.38 or newer (Ubuntu 24.04, Debian 13,
Fedora 39, Linux Mint 22 or later), X11 or XWayland, and ALSA, JACK or
PipeWire for audio. The plugins work in any VST3 or LV2 host, for example
Ardour, Reaper, Bitwig Studio, Carla or Qtractor.

Install the plugins
-------------------
Option A: open a terminal in this folder and run
    ./install.sh
It copies the plugins to ~/.vst3 and ~/.lv2 (no administrator rights needed)
and replaces an older version if there is one.

Option B: copy the folders by hand:
    "Clean Tone Studio.vst3"  into  ~/.vst3/
    "Clean Tone Studio.lv2"   into  ~/.lv2/

Then rescan plugins in your DAW and insert Clean Tone Studio on your guitar
track. It reports its latency (5 samples, about 0.1 ms; 3 ms more with
LOOKAHEAD), so the DAW compensates. Use either the VST3 or the LV2, not both
on one track: they sound the same. The LV2 always runs in stereo; on a mono
guitar track your DAW feeds the guitar to both inputs, which gives the same
sound.

Use the standalone app
----------------------
Run Standalone/CleanToneStudio (double-click it, or start it from a
terminal: ./CleanToneStudio). If your file manager opens it as a file,
right-click it and make it executable, or run:
    chmod +x Standalone/CleanToneStudio
  1. Choose your audio interface under Options > Audio/MIDI Settings...
     (JACK gives the lowest latency; ALSA "default" goes through
     PulseAudio/PipeWire). Turn on only the input your guitar is plugged into.
  2. Untick "Mute audio input" (it is on by default to avoid speaker feedback).
  3. Pick a preset (top right) and play.
The app puts your guitar in the center with the delay and reverb in stereo
around it, and remembers your audio settings and last sound.

Your presets are kept in ~/Documents/Clean Tone Studio/Presets (or your
language's Documents folder), shared by the app and the plugins, so presets
from Windows can be copied in there too.

Uninstall
---------
Delete ~/.vst3/Clean Tone Studio.vst3, ~/.lv2/Clean Tone Studio.lv2 and the
app. Your presets stay in ~/Documents/Clean Tone Studio/Presets.
