CLEAN TONE STUDIO {VERSION} - Quick Start (Windows 64-bit)
==========================================================

What's in this folder
---------------------
  Standalone\Clean Tone Studio.exe   The standalone app: play your guitar straight
                                     through your ASIO sound card (no install needed)
  VST3\Clean Tone Studio.vst3        The same thing as a plugin for Cubase or another
                                     DAW (a folder - keep it whole)
  Install VST3.bat                   Copies the plugin into C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3
  README.md                          Full manual: signal chain, every control, tests
  MEASUREMENTS.md                    Measured distortion, aliasing, response, latency, CPU
  LICENSE.txt                        GNU Affero General Public License version 3
  THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt            Licenses for included components

Both run on any 64-bit Windows 10/11 PC. There's nothing else to install.

New in 1.5.0 - four controls for a clearer sound (all off by default)
------------
* Clarity (next to Warmth): trims the muddy low mids only when they build up - chords,
  the neck pickup, a boomy cabinet - and leaves a balanced tone alone. Try 40-70 %.
* Pick (in SUSTAIN / DYNAMICS): turn up for more snap on each note, down for a softer,
  rounder start. The rest of the note isn't changed. Handy with a lot of Sustain.
* TIGHT (button next to TONE & CABINET): keeps the deep lows out of Warmth, so it adds
  fullness without the low end getting boomy. Great with humbuckers or lots of Warmth.
* Reverb Ducking: the reverb backs off while you play and blooms in the gaps.
* Your existing presets and projects sound exactly as before.

WHAT'S IN IT (in signal order)
------------------------------
* Input: a Pickup selector (Single Coil, Humbucker or Flat). Each setting applies its own
  EQ correction and level adjustment, so both pickup types hit the compressor the same way.
  There's also a 50/60 Hz hum filter for single coils and a soft noise gate.

* Sustain: a smooth optical-style compressor that lets go more slowly the harder it's
  working, with a long gentle tail after sustained playing, and that doesn't over-squash
  low notes. It has automatic make-up gain, an Attack control that lets the pick attack
  through, and a Blend control that mixes in the uncompressed signal to keep detail.
  LOOKAHEAD catches the sharpest pick attacks smoothly (3 ms latency). An ON switch turns
  it off if you already use a hardware compressor before your interface. It fades smoothly
  without clicking, and changing presets never changes ON or LOOKAHEAD.

* Warmth: a tube-style stage that adds fullness, more on low notes than high ones, and
  blooms as you dig in. Clean and smooth at low settings, richer as you turn it up.
  TIGHT keeps the deep lows out of it. Clarity trims low-mid mud only when it builds up.

* Tone: Bass, Mid with adjustable frequency, Presence, Treble, Low Cut and High Cut.

* Cabinet: three speaker-cabinet voicings (Open 1x12, Classic 2x12, Closed 4x12), an IR
  Loader for your own impulse responses (two slots, A and B, with a blend and ALIGN), or
  Off (DI).

* Delay: repeats get darker each time, time changes glide like tape instead of clicking,
  plus subtle modulation in Sine or Tape style, ducking (repeats drop back while you
  play), ping-pong and tempo sync (the project tempo in Cubase; your own tempo or TAP in
  the app).

* Reverb: early reflections and a smooth, dense 16-line tail with no metallic ringing.
  Ducking lets it back off while you play.
  Decay is the real decay time; the highs fade faster, like a real room. The low cut is
  set to 200 Hz by default so it doesn't get muddy.

* Output: an Output level control, input and output meters, and Auto Level. Auto Level
  keeps the output as loud as your dry guitar whenever you change presets or settings, so
  a sound never seems better just because it's louder. With it on, the presets are within
  1 dB of each other, compared with about 7 dB apart without it. The Output knob is the
  only control that changes the final volume.

* Presets: 8 starting points, including Glassy Single Coil, Warm Jazz Humbucker, Endless
  Sustain, Ambient Swell and DI Clean (No Cab). You can also save, replace and delete your
  own presets with the Save and Delete buttons (see YOUR OWN PRESETS below). The app and
  the VST3 share the same presets.

Formats:
* VST3 plugin for Cubase and other DAWs.
* Standalone app that plays through an ASIO sound card. It puts your guitar in the center
  with the delay and reverb in stereo around it, and remembers your sound card and settings.


STANDALONE APP (ASIO)
---------------------
1. Install your sound card's ASIO driver (from the manufacturer's website) and connect the card.
   Close Cubase and anything else that uses the card.

2. Copy "Standalone\Clean Tone Studio.exe" anywhere you like (e.g. the Desktop) and
   double-click it.
   The first time, Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" because the app isn't
   code-signed. Click "More info", then "Run anyway".

3. Click  Options -> Audio/MIDI Settings...
     - Audio device type:      ASIO
     - Device:                 your sound card's ASIO driver
     - Active input channels:  turn on ONLY the input your guitar is plugged into
     - Active output channels: your headphone / monitor outputs
     - Buffer size:            64-128 samples (raise it if you hear crackles)
     - "Control Panel" opens the sound card's own driver settings.

4. Untick "Mute audio input". The app mutes input by default to prevent speaker
   feedback, which isn't a risk with a guitar and headphones. It remembers this.

5. Pick a preset (top right) and play. Single coils: start with "Glassy Single Coil".
   Humbuckers: start with "Warm Jazz Humbucker" or "Humbucker Chime".

The app remembers your sound card, settings and last sound every time you open it.

No sound?
  - Check that "Mute audio input" is unticked and that the IN meter moves when you play.
  - Check that the correct input channel is ticked under Active input channels.
  - Windows Settings -> Privacy & security -> Microphone: turn on
    "Let desktop apps access your microphone" (some drivers need this).


VST3 PLUGIN FOR CUBASE (optional)
---------------------------------
Close Cubase, then either:
  A: double-click "Install VST3.bat" and click Yes when Windows asks for administrator
     permission, or
  B: copy the whole "VST3\Clean Tone Studio.vst3" folder into
         C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3
Then start Cubase. Find it in the track's Inserts by typing "Clean Tone".


YOUR OWN PRESETS
----------------
- Get a sound you like, click "Save" (next to the preset menu), type a name, press Enter.
- Your presets appear in the preset menu under "My Presets". They're shared between the app
  and the VST3 in Cubase, so a sound you save in one shows up in the other.
- "(edited)" after the name means you've moved knobs since loading it. Pick the preset again
  to go back to it, or Save to keep the changes (same name = replace).
- "Delete" removes the selected one of your presets (click it twice to confirm). It goes to
  the Recycle Bin, so you can get it back.
- Presets never change Output, Auto Level, the delay's tempo, or the compressor's ON and
  LOOKAHEAD switches.
- If you use the IR Loader, your preset keeps the IRs inside it, so it works on another PC
  even without the IR files.
- They're stored in  Documents\Clean Tone Studio\Presets  (one .ctpreset file each).
  To move them to another PC, copy that folder.


TIPS
----
- Aim for guitar peaks of about -12 dB on the IN meter. If the GR meter shows constant
  heavy compression, turn Input down.
- Auto Level (on by default) keeps your volume the same when you change presets or knobs.
  Use Output to set how loud the app is.
- Already using a hardware compressor before your interface? Click the ON button in the
  SUSTAIN / DYNAMICS panel to switch the plugin's compressor off. Presets won't turn it back on.
- Double-click any knob to reset it.
- Window too small or too big? Drag its bottom-right corner; everything scales.


UPGRADING FROM 1.6.0 ("OPEN CLEAN TONE STUDIO")
-----------------------------------------------
It's the same plugin, back under its original name. Your Cubase projects load it as
before. Presets you saved with 1.6.0 (in Documents\Open Clean Tone Studio\Presets) are
copied back to Documents\Clean Tone Studio\Presets the first time you open it; none of
your other presets are replaced, and the 1.6.0 folder is left as it was. Install VST3.bat
removes "Open Clean Tone Studio.vst3" so Cubase doesn't list the plugin twice; if you
install by hand, delete that folder yourself. The app goes back to the sound card
settings you had before 1.6.0. You can delete "Open Clean Tone Studio.exe".


UNINSTALL
---------
Delete C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\Clean Tone Studio.vst3 and the .exe.
Your presets are kept in Documents\Clean Tone Studio\Presets.
